# WAXBALL Support

Public service: https://waxball.akra.kr/

- Product and technical support: akradev001@gmail.com
- Privacy requests: akradev001@gmail.com
- Local-data deletion: use the operating system's app-data controls or uninstall
  WAXBALL.
- Advertising privacy choices: open WAXBALL Settings when the privacy-options
  entry is available, and use the operating system's advertising controls where
  applicable.

When contacting support, include the WAXBALL version, platform version, and a
short description. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or private keys.
